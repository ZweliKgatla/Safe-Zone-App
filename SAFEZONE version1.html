How it works
  1. ① PAIR

    Pair the watch

    Strap the SafeZone watch and tag onto your child. It connects over cellular in minutes — no setup on their end.

  2. ② SET

    Set a safe distance

    Choose how far is too far. You'll get an alert the moment your child crosses that line.

  3. ③ MONITOR

    Get notified

    Live location on the map, plus an instant alert if they leave the zone, press SOS, or lose signal.
