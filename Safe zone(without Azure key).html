About SafeZone

A safety net you can wear, clip on and carry

SafeZone is a personal safety platform made up of a smartwatch, a smart tag and a mobile app, backed by an AI assistant.

What SafeZone is

SafeZone connects an off-the-shelf smartwatch and smart tag to one app. You draw safe zones on a map, and the moment someone leaves them, presses SOS or loses signal, you are told. No smartphone is needed for the person wearing the watch.

The problem it solves

Families, carers and employers can't be everywhere. Children walk to school alone, elderly relatives wander, and lone workers face risk with nobody checking in. Phone calls and messages only work if the person can answer. SafeZone tells you what's happening without needing them to do anything.

Why it was created

SafeZone began as a first-year ICT project at Tshwane University of Technology. We wanted to build something real: a product that combines hardware, software and AI to solve a problem we and our families see every day.

Why it matters in South Africa

Many families live far from workplaces and schools, and rely on walking, taxis and shared transport. High levels of violent crime and thousands of missing-person reports each year mean peace of mind isn't a luxury. We are building for local needs: SMS fallback for patchy data, rand pricing, and POPIA-aware consent.

How it works

Six steps from sign-up to peace of mind

Setup takes about five minutes. No technical skills needed.

  1. Create an account

    Sign up in the app with your email and choose a plan. Free is always available.

  2. Add family members or devices

    Pair a watch or smart tag by holding it near your phone. Each person gets a profile.

  3. Set safe zones

    Draw zones on the map for home, school or work, and choose a safe distance.

  4. Receive alerts

    If someone leaves a safe zone, goes too far or the battery runs low, you get a push alert, with SMS as a backup.

  5. Use SOS in an emergency

    The wearer holds the SOS button for 3 seconds. Live location and an alarm are sent at once.

  6. Trusted contacts are notified

    Your chosen emergency contacts get the alert too, so help can reach them even if you can't.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Use the contact form below.

Does SafeZone work offline?

The watch and tag keep working when your phone is offline, because the watch uses its own cellular connection. If the watch has no data, alerts fall back to SMS. In places with no network at all, the last known location is saved and shown as soon as signal returns.

How accurate is location tracking?

Outdoors with a clear view of the sky, GPS is typically accurate to within a few metres. Indoors, in tall buildings or under heavy tree cover it can be less precise, and SafeZone uses Wi-Fi and cell-tower data to help. The app shows an accuracy circle so you can see how sure the location is.

Can I track multiple people?

Yes. Free covers one person or tag, Family up to five, Premium up to ten, and Business supports teams with a shared dashboard.

Is my data private?

Yes. Location data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only the guardians you invite can see it. We never sell location data. Tracking an adult needs their consent, in line with POPIA, and they can see who has access.

What happens if there's no internet?

SafeZone sends alerts by SMS instead, so you still hear about SOS presses and zone alerts. The full map and history return when data is available again.

Does my child need a smartphone?

No. The child wears a smartwatch with the smart tag. Only the guardian needs the SafeZone app on a phone.

Which phones does the app support?

SafeZone is planned for both Android and iPhone from a single cross-platform app.
